Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

KidsVideosUploader ("the app") is a private desktop tool operated by Ammar Othman, the owner of the Lulu & Momo YouTube channel. The app is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, subtitles, thumbnails, "made for kids" setting and publish time, on the owner's channel only.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share any data about viewers or any other YouTube users.
  • It does not display YouTube data to anyone, and it has no users other than the channel owner.
  • It does not sell or transfer any data to third parties.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
